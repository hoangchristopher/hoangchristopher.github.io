A.2: Recover Homographies

Here are the correspondances for each image set visualized.

As for the homographies, we will solve for them using this system of equations where a point (x, y) on the "before" image corresponds to a point (u, v) on the "after" image. The resulting vector, when reshaped to 3x3 with the element in the 3rd row and 3rd column equalling a constant scaling factor, will give us our 3x3 homography matrix.

A.4: Blend the Images into a Mosaic"

To blend two images together, I first computed the homography required to warp the correspondances from the first image to the second image. Then, I used this homography to warp the first image. From there, I created a mask that follows the new shape of the warped first image, and then used a Laplacian pyramid to blend the warped first image and the second image.
